# YouRoll — kept out of the first port

Features of YouRec intentionally left out of the first Android pass (recorder + basic editor).
Each entry names the Swift sources so it can be picked up later. Keep this list current:
whenever something is skipped or simplified, add it here.

## Editor tools (not ported yet)

- [~] Video effects (editor): the typed model and catalogue with the Swift JSON (`VideoEffectModel.kt`, `VideoEffectCatalog.kt`, `VideoEffectDefinitions` is the shader index table there; unknown kinds skipped, canonical bounds on decode), `VideoEffectMath.kt` (Oklab, palettes, cadence clock, crash geometry, temporal windows, background placement), `VideoEffectTiming.kt` (`sequenceRate`, `mutesSourceAudio`, `effectPlaybackSpans`, `TimelineEditing.freezing`), `EffectAnimation.kt`/`EffectAnimationControls.kt` (A/B endpoints for Color Correction), keyframes on every animatable control, the palette parts of `VideoEffectAssets.kt` with `assets/VideoEffects/PopPalettes.json`, a GLSL ES 3.00 port of the editor kernels of `VideoEffects.metal` (`VideoEffectsShaders.kt`) run by one GL renderer (`MetalVideoEffects.kt`: `MetalEffectRenderer`, the Media3 `VideoEffectsGlEffect`), the Video effects screen (`VideoEffectsEditor.kt`: applied list with on/off and delete, library sections, inspector with sliders, pickers, steppers, keyframe diamonds and navigation, Animate, help, Color Correction Light/Color panel with A/B animation, Original/Effects comparison, Save/Lock palette), tool regions, chips, "In this project", request/removal, validation. The same renderer draws the export (after framing and Mirror, before overlays and placement), the preview (installed on every player while the edit has effects, after the colour grade) and each participant of a transition window, so preview == export. Holds play a "hold clip" (the held source frame repeated at 30 fps, `VideoEffectSampling.kt`) in both, so Push-in and Print grain animate. Done: Color Correction, RGB Offset, Negative Strobe, Silent Tint, Three-Strip, Bleach Bypass, Day for Night, Color Isolation (a colour), Noir, Expressionism, Quantization (scene, Pop, B&W, saved and locked palettes, smoothing, dithering), Crash Zoom (manual target), Kaleidoscope, Widescreen 1953, Freeze Frame, Undercranking (cadence, speed-up, flicker, weave, mute), Halation, 16mm, Super 8; Step Printing's retiming. Left out or simplified:
  - [ ] Effects that need Apple Vision/Core ML segmentation, depth or a clean plate are kept in projects (model, JSON, validation) and listed in the library as "Not yet available"; the export refuses them with a message (`VideoEffectSupport`): Auto Background, Dolly Zoom, Chroma Key (needs its background media, clean plate and temporal key matte too), Color Isolation's "The subject" mode, Quantization's Face detail (ignored, as without a person mask on iOS), Widescreen's oval bokeh (hidden without depth, as on iOS), Crash Zoom's Follow face (new Crash Zooms start with it off; a project with it on is refused): `VideoEffectSubjectTracking.swift`, `VideoEffectDepth.swift`, `VideoEffectCleanPlate.swift`, `VideoEffectCaptureAnalysis.swift`, the person/mask parts of `VideoEffectAssets.swift` (`VideoEffectInputs`, `VideoEffectPersonProvider`), `MetalVideoEffects.swift` (`VideoEffectMaskSmoother`, `VideoEffectMatteCache`, `VideoEffectLiveMatteHistory`), the mask/depth/key kernels of `VideoEffects.metal`
  - [ ] Night fix (all modes; Medium/Precise need optical flow): `NightFix.swift`, the `nf_*` kernels
  - [ ] Step Printing's frame blocks and smear (`VideoEffectSamplePlan`, `VideoEffectFrameIndex`/`VideoEffectFrameAnalysis`, `VideoEffectSourceReader`): its speed retimes the clip, but the effect is "Not yet available" and refused by the export, because the blend needs the frames after the current one, which a streaming Media3 effect does not have: `VideoEffectSampling.swift`
  - [ ] Color Isolation and Chroma Key's temporal matte (`algorithm` 2, the EMA over source-frame mattes, `VideoEffectSourceMatteProvider`): Color Isolation computes the hue weight per frame (the algorithm 1 look) and keeps `algorithm` 2 in the document: `VideoEffectSampling.swift`, `MetalVideoEffects.swift`
  - [ ] Quantization's scene palette is analysed from the frames the renderer draws, every 0.2 s of source time with the track's smoothing, instead of a persisted palette track scanned from the clip start (`VideoEffectAnalysis.palette`): after a seek, and at the start of a split piece in the export, the smoothing starts afresh. "Save palette from this frame" and "Lock palette" take the palette analysed for the paused frame; the analysed image is reduced on the GPU and read back as 8-bit sRGB (iOS: float linear)
  - [ ] Cadence holds (Undercranking, Super 8) repeat the first frame the effect sees in each cadence group; after a seek into a group the preview shows the seek frame for the rest of that group (iOS decodes the group's first frame). Inside a transition window the group's frame is the one at or before its boundary (iOS: at or after)
  - [ ] The preview finds each frame's clip from its presentation time on the playlist clock, and, while the edit has effects, frames every picture in GL (crop or fit, Mirror) and shows it canvas-sized; switching into or out of that mode, and installing the effect, stops and reloads the players. Effect-look changes keep the playlist and redraw the paused frame; time effects and holds rebuild it. Hold clips (`Cache/hold-*.mp4`, 10 s at 30 fps, one per held picture) are an extra H.264 generation, are prepared before the hold plays ("Preparing effects"; until then the hold plays the slowed frame without its effects) and have no eviction: `EditorController.kt`, `VideoEffectSampling.kt`
  - [ ] The working space is linear sRGB in RGBA16F textures (RGBA8 when the GPU lacks half-float colour buffers); HDR sources are tone-mapped to SDR by Media3 before the effects (iOS's `ve_toneMap` shoulder is not ported); the grain lattice is generated per frame like Metal's `ve_grainNoise`, but GLSL random and blur rounding differ from Metal at the last bit: `MetalVideoEffects.swift`
  - [ ] Freeze Frame's "Continue" and "Fade out over hold" audio modes: a hold is silent in every mode (Cut is the default): `MediaRenderer.swift` (`appendSourceAudio`), `VideoEffectBackgroundAudio.swift`
  - [ ] Editor UI: the Kaleidoscope guide (drag the centre and handle, pan and twist the source; `KaleidoscopeGuide.swift`) and the RGB Offset pads (`RGBOffsetPads.swift`) are replaced by their sliders; colour pickers (eyedropper "Pick" sampling, the system `ColorPicker`) are not offered (Color Isolation keeps its hue slider); background media import and "Position in preview"; the slow-effect sample window, refinement and Stop/Preview (`EffectPreviewWindow`, `EffectApplicationReceipt`, `VideoEffectPreparation`); swipe-to-delete in the applied list is a trash button; accessibility-size layouts: `VideoEffectsEditor.swift`, `EffectApplication.swift`
  - [ ] Live recorder effects stay out (see Recorder): `LiveVideoEffects.swift`, `LiveEffectsPanel.swift`, `RGBOffsetPads.swift`, `KaleidoscopeGuide.swift` capture guides, `CaptureZoomHistory.swift`, `DollyZoomFollower`/`VideoEffectLiveBudget` in `VideoEffectMath.swift`, `VideoEffectsDiagnostics.swift`
- [x] Color LUTs and colour grading: typed `ColorSettings`/`ColorLook` with the Swift keys (VideoTools.kt), the strict CUBE reader, hash-named LUT payloads (`LUTAssets`) and the grade library with favourites and saved grades (ColorGrading.kt, `ColorLibrary` in the app's files directory), the Apple Log curves, primaries and Highlights (LogColor.kt), `ColorLUT` with the 14 built-in looks (VideoToolCompositor.kt), the Color tool (`ToolWorkspace` in EditorTools.kt, ColorGradeControls.kt: strength, Highlights, exposure and white balance, Built-in/Imported/Favorites/Grades with thumbnails of the paused frame, LUT import from the document picker, "LUT expects", Save grade, Remove look), tool regions, chips, "In this project", Undo/Redo. Left out or simplified:
  - [ ] Log footage: no Log capture or Log source detection on Android (`LogColor.profile` answers null), so the Log viewing transform, local highlight recovery and `LogThumbnailSource` never run; a grade's recovery value is kept and shown as "saved but does not affect this footage".   - [ ] White balance: `CITemperatureAndTint` is approximated by a Bradford adaptation between the DNG SDK temperature/tint white points (6500 K → 6500 + 2500·warmth K, tint ×100); exact Core Image numbers are unverified: `VideoToolCompositor.swift` (`ColorLUT.apply`)

  - [ ] Preview timing: the preview grade follows the playlist item the player reports (`onMediaItemTransition`, seeks), so the first frame after a seek or cut can show the neighbour's grade; ExoPlayer takes video effects only when its renderer is enabled again, so the first grade of a session stops and reloads the players. The export is frame exact: `EditorController.kt`
